Today is a very special milestone for me. I want to introduce **enkihost**, a project born from an aspiration that has lived with me for a long time: to build my own home in the cloud, while simultaneously offering that reliable haven to others.

<!--more-->

### Starting Humble and Focused

Like every great journey, enkihost starts with a steady, humble first step. In this initial phase, we are exclusively dedicated to providing **hosting for static sites built with Jekyll** straight from public Git repositories. It is the purest, most efficient way to publish static content today, making it the ideal foundation to stress-test and validate our core infrastructure.

### The Road Ahead: Rails and Sinatra

While we begin with static deployments, the engine behind enkihost is engineered for dynamic expansion. Very soon, we will introduce full runtime support for dynamic Ruby applications with **Ruby on Rails** and **Sinatra**. My vision is to forge an environment where deploying a Ruby application feels as effortless, fast, and frictionless as it always should have been.

### Complete Pricing Transparency

Regarding pricing, I want to maintain total transparency: we are currently running in a controlled early launch. While you might see tier guidelines across the platform, right now **only the "Free Tier" is active**. Plan upgrades are temporarily locked because I prioritize bulletproof stability before scaling out capacity. It's my commitment to craftsmanship and to those placing their trust in this project from day one.

### A Personal Dream Realized

I have always wanted to architect a hosting platform tailored to my own workflows. I wanted something that reflects how I believe the modern web should be: simple, transparent, and sovereign. Yet as I designed it, I realized this need wasn't mine alone.

Now I have the opportunity to build something that fellow developers and makers will genuinely enjoy using. **Enkihost** is more than just Linux servers and automation scripts; it is the concrete expression of a desire to serve and build enduring tools for the developer community.

Thank you for being here at the start. This is only the beginning!
