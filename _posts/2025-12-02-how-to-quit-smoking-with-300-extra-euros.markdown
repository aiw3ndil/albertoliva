Some time ago I made one of the best decisions of my life: quitting smoking. It wasn't just a health choice, but a radical transformation of my personal finances. When I sat down and ran the numbers, I realized that **I now have about €300 extra in my pocket every single month**.

<!--more-->

## The True Cost of Smoking

When I smoked, I never stopped to calculate what I was actually spending. A pack here, another there... it seemed like small change at the time. But the reality is eye-opening:

- **Average price of a pack in Finland:** approximately €10
- **Daily consumption:** 1 pack
- **Monthly spend:** €10 × 30 days = **€300**

Three hundred euros a month. Three thousand six hundred euros a year. Money literally going up in smoke.

## Why is it Different in Finland?

Living in Finland proved to be a decisive factor in my choice. Here, cigarettes are remarkably expensive, much more so than in Spain. In addition, Finnish culture doesn't normalize the smoking habit as much as in other countries. Fewer people smoke, designated smoking areas are rare, and the weather isn't exactly inviting when it's -20°C outside.

All of this builds an atmosphere that genuinely nudges you toward quitting.

## The Transformation: Far Beyond Just the Money

While €300 a month is a tremendous motivator, the benefits extend far beyond the wallet:

### Health
- **Breathing:** Within the first few weeks, you notice a dramatic improvement in lung capacity.
- **Energy:** Less fatigue, more vitality throughout the day.
- **Taste and smell:** You rediscover nuances in food you had long forgotten.

### Quality of Life
- **Freedom:** No longer dependent on stepping outside every two hours to smoke.
- **Time:** All the time previously wasted smoking is now truly yours.
- **Self-esteem:** The pride of taking back control is incredible.

### Personal Finances
Those €300 every month are now invested into:
- Saving for future projects
- Funding hobbies and activities I genuinely enjoy
- Treating myself to little luxuries I couldn't previously justify

## Tips for Anyone Looking to Quit

If you're contemplating quitting smoking, here is what worked for me:

1. **Pinpoint your motivation:** Whether health, financial freedom, or personal autonomy, find your bedrock reason.
2. **Pick your moment:** It doesn't need to be perfect, but avoiding peak-stress periods helps.
3. **Re-engineer your routines:** Identify when you smoke most and substitute those behavioral triggers.
4. **Track your savings:** Watching the daily savings pile up in an account is deeply gratifying.
5. **Seek support:** Whether friends, family, or cessation professionals, you don't have to carry it alone.

## Final Reflection

Quitting smoking was one of the finest gifts I've ever given myself. The extra €300 a month is great, but what has truly transformed my life is the regained sense of sovereignty over my habits.

I'm no longer bound to a habit that was dragging me down on every front. Today I am free, healthier, and happier. And my bank account agrees.

If you're thinking about quitting, let this be your nudge. Your future self will thank you.
