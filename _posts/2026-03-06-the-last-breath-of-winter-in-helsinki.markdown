There is a distinct sound across Helsinki these days: the incessant dripping of icicles surrendering to the sun and the crunch of sidewalk ice beginning to yield. For many, it heralds springtime hope; for me, it feels like a farewell I am reluctant to accept.

<!--more-->

### The Beauty of White Stillness

I love winter. I don't mean merely the postcard aesthetic of snow-draped pines in Central Park, but what winter does to the human spirit. There is a brutal, refreshing honesty in -20°C temperatures that compels you to be fully present—to feel every breath as a small, quiet victory of life.

Helsinki blankets itself in silence, intimacy, and wonder under the snow. The pure white unifies the landscape, softening urban edges and gifting us that ethereal, blue-tinted northern twilight. It is the season of deep introspection, of cradling a steaming cup of coffee, and of a stillness found only when the bustling world halts beneath a crystalline mantle.

### The Rumble of the Thaw

Walking the streets today, I watched the underlying asphalt greys begin to peek through. The slush turns murky, runoff rushes down street gutters, and the pristine purity of January fades by the hour. I know spring brings its own renewal, that endless daylight will soon arrive, and that nature will burst forward in full vigor—yet I cannot shake a tender sense of melancholy.

I don't want it to end. I don't want to pack away my heavy woolen coat or say goodbye to the crunch of fresh snow beneath my boots. There is something in winter's defiance, in its refusal to be tamed, that captivates me. It is the season that challenges us most, yet gently wraps us in its quiet, shared solitude.

### Embracing the Cycle (or Trying To)

Nietzsche spoke of *Amor Fati*—loving fate and cherishing every beat of the eternal cycle. I suppose that love entails accepting that winter must perish so that everything else may flourish anew. But today, gazing at the receding snow on my windowsill, I choose to linger just a little longer in this comforting cold.

Helsinki is stirring awake, but my heart still wishes to slumber a while longer under the snow.

> *"In the midst of winter, I found there was, within me, an invincible summer."*
> — *Albert Camus* (Though today, honestly, I wish my winter were the invincible one).
