Today I want to share a very special moment in my life: my new chapter in Finland has officially begun!

<!--more-->

I am now registered with the Finnish social security system, which gives me peace of mind and support as I adapt to this country. Having this safety net is essential for feeling secure and being able to focus on what matters most: learning, growing, and building a life here.

And that's not all: I have a new apartment thanks to HEKA! For those unfamiliar, HEKA is the main social housing provider in Helsinki, offering affordable apartments to people who, like me, are starting fresh here. Having a place of my own provides stability and a spot where I can truly feel at home as I move forward on this path.

Furthermore, I am receiving financial support for studying Finnish. This means I can dedicate myself to learning the language worry-free, explore the culture more deeply, and begin to fully integrate into Finnish society.

I feel that all of this is an incredible opportunity for a fresh start. Having a home, social security, and time to study Finnish allows me to build a solid foundation for my life in Finland. Every single day is one step closer to integration and achieving my goals.

I'm excited for what's to come: learning, meeting people, integrating, and making the most of this experience. This stage isn't just a change of scenery; it's a true reset filled with possibilities and new opportunities.
