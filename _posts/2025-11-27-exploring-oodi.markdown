Today we had the opportunity to visit the Oodi library with our course group, and I must say it was a fascinating experience. Oodi is not just a library; it is a vibrant, modern public space that brings reading, technology, and culture together under one roof.

<!--more-->

From the moment you walk in, the architecture is striking: enormous glass windows flooding the space with natural light, open layouts, and cozy lounge spots that invite you to stay and explore. The library holds more than 100,000 books, but what truly makes it special are its modern, accessible services open to all.

Among the things that caught our attention most were:

* **Work and study spaces:** quiet rooms with individual desks, group study pods, and comfortable reading nooks.
* **Technology and creativity:** Oodi offers 3D printers, VR stations, musical instruments, and recording studios.
* **Culture and entertainment:** spaces for movie screenings, live music, workshops, and exhibitions—all open to the public.
* **Children's area:** a colorful, welcoming environment where kids can read and learn through play.

Moreover, everything is designed for accessibility: ramps, elevators, and digital resources that can be accessed remotely from home. I was genuinely amazed by the sheer quantity of free activities the library offers to the community.

For us, as students of the Finnish language, it was an excellent spot to practice, discover books and magazines, and witness how a public facility can feel so futuristic yet warm and welcoming. Oodi isn't just a place to read; it is a hub of culture, learning, and creativity.

Leaving the tour left us eager to return, explore more of its services, and take full advantage of everything this incredible library brings to the city of Helsinki.
